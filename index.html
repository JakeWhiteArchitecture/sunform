Free sun hours and solar shading analysis for designers

Import an IFC or BIM model and see direct sunlight hours across roofs, ground and facades as a smooth 3D heatmap.

SunForm is a free, browser-based solar analysis tool for architects, architectural technologists and designers. Load a building model, pick a location and season, and run a sun hours study to understand daylight, solar exposure and solar shading across a scheme. Everything runs in your browser, with no login and nothing to install.

  • Sun hours analysis on roofs, ground surfaces and vertical facades
  • Solar shading and shadow studies driven by real sun paths
  • Direct IFC and BIM model import via web-ifc
  • Smooth, per-pixel 3D sunlight heatmap with no visible grid
  • Seasonal comparison across winter, spring, autumn and summer
  • Export coloured GLB models and dimensioned PDF plans
Designed for

Domestic-scale solar studies, extending to small commercial and public projects. Built for architects, architectural technologists, surveyors and designers who can critically interpret and verify the results.
